Live — from the wheel & your attacks

    How damage is calculated

    No numbers on this page are invented. Every raid figure is derived from the same public Squeekchain API that other Genesis projects use.

    STEP 1

    The hamster runs

    A wheel sensor (RP2040 + A3144E) records the physical wheel's rotations and turns them into distance (mm) and work units. Latest status: –, total distance –.

    STEP 2

    10-minute rounds

    Every 10 minutes, one round closes. That round's work units are accepted by the oracle and settled to the Mining contract on Sepolia. Current last settled round: –.

    STEP 3

    Block progress = boss HP

    At the live rate, – work units complete 1,000 blocks. Full progress of 1,000,000,000 = the boss falls and a new block is born. Current progress: –.

    STEP 4

    Weekly reset

    Your weekly totals (damage, attacks, best hit, wheel meters) are saved in your browser and reset every Monday 00:00 UTC. The boss itself is whichever block is in progress — a new boss about once a minute — so no two visits ever face quite the same boss.
